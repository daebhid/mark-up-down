Open a markdown file and read it one sentence at a time.

The arrow keys move you through it. Left and right step a sentence, up and down jump further.

Nothing is uploaded and your document is opened read-only, so the app can never write to it.

Tip Me
mark↑↓
Cmd+Enter saves · Esc closes

→ and ← step forward and back one viewport at a time.

↓ and ↑ jump, by paragraph or by a set number of words.

The bookmark keeps your place. Switch it off and the document always opens at the top.

The pencil, or Enter, opens a note on the sentence you're reading. Notes append to a markdown file of your choosing, in the order you write them.
